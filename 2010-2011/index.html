EMToast: The Absurd Universe Takes Shape (2010–2011) | Podcast Deep Dive

By EMToast Staff
June 20, 2026

EMToast: The Absurd Universe Takes Shape (2010–2011) continues the deep dive into the strange evolution of EMToast, exploring how the site’s humor expanded into a fully formed absurd news universe. This episode covers the rise of surreal headlines, recurring themes like distrust of authority, celebrity distortions, bizarre animal motifs, and the emergence of internet “mythology” through characters and running jokes that blurred the line between satire and reality.

From robot babies and reality TV parodies to foaming horses, fake science, and the infamous Sick Junior mystery, EMToast begins to feel less like a collection of jokes and more like a strange, self-contained world shaped by early internet culture and post-recession skepticism.
