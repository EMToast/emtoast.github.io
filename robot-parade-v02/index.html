Robot Parade v.02

By EMToast Staff
December 11, 2022

It was a bright and sunny day in the bustling city, and the annual robot parade was just getting underway. The streets were packed with people, all eager to catch a glimpse of the impressive machines on display.

As the parade made its way down Main Street, the robots shimmered in the afternoon sun. There were robots of all shapes and sizes, from tiny ones no bigger than a bug to massive ones that towered over the buildings.

But among the crowd, there was a lone figure who stood out from the rest. It was a rugged, no-nonsense detective named Jack Hunter, and he had been hired to investigate a string of strange occurrences at the parade.

Hunter pushed his way through the throngs of people, his keen eyes scanning the crowd for any sign of trouble. But as the parade continued, he couldn't shake the feeling that something was off.

Suddenly, a loud crash echoed through the streets, and the crowd erupted into chaos. Hunter quickly spotted the source of the commotion: one of the robots had gone haywire and was wreaking havoc on the parade.

Without hesitation, Hunter sprang into action. He dodged and weaved through the panicked crowd, his gun drawn and ready. When he finally caught up to the rogue robot, he took aim and fired a single, well-placed shot that shut it down for good.

The crowd cheered as Hunter emerged victorious, and the parade continued without further incident. But Hunter knew that this was just the beginning. He had a feeling that there was more to this case than met the eye, and he was determined to get to the bottom of it.

As the sun set on the city and the robot parade came to an end, Hunter disappeared into the shadows, ready to continue his investigation and bring the culprits to justice.
