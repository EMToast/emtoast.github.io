Forget Action Park. This is EMToast. We have several different permits! 

By EMToast Staff
November 10, 2026

Welcome to EM Toast Adventure Park

Located in scenic Somerset, Montana, EM Toast Adventure Park promises an amusement experience unlike any other. Visitors can explore attractions such as the Diarrhea Log Flume, the Nipple Slide, and the mysterious Assholder Tower.

The park's management proudly promotes its unconventional approach to entertainment, but local officials have a few concerns. Between questionable permits, undocumented structures, mutant creatures, and a giant flaming Santa, there's apparently quite a bit that still needs explaining.

Perhaps most concerning is the park's reported safety testing: a grand total of three minutes.

Admission is reasonably priced, but whether you'll make it home in one piece is another question entirely. Welcome to EM Toast, where adventure comes first and safety testing is apparently an afterthought.
