Uncle Brian Dies in Jail

By EMToast Staff
November 13, 2020

Uncle Brian died in jail last night. His sister in law and his wife could not post bail. They got no money. Cause of death -- he bet his cellmate he could inhale 6 peanuts. He snorted 4 nuts, a pea, and a corn kernel. The corns went directly into his lung and he choked. Brian quoted Shakespear on his death bad. Nevermore ravens. Never more amma gonna die.
