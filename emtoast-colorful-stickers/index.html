Emtoast Colorful Stickers

By EMToast Staff
December 11, 2022

Feel free to print out these colorful stickers and stick them all over your kitchen. Back in the 80's kids would have loved these kind of stickers for their sticker books. One kid told me his favorite one was the weird dinosaur one filled with color changing oil. I have no clue what that kid meant or who he was. Is there any point to making sense anymore? I don't think there is. How long can we keep this up for? More toast images. Toast or die!
