EMToa.st Printable 80's Style Newspaper Flyer

By EMToast Staff
June 8, 2026

Everybody and his brother are making AI advertisements now.  Your local flea market has individual ads for each vendor.  Your boss is bypassing the marketing department and making their own custom hanging banners for customer visits.  Local businesses and organizations used to have that home town 1997 Microsoft Publisher look.  Now it looks like they are pulled out of the latest video game. You can spot them from 100 miles away. No white space, emoji's for bullet points, way too much detail for any desktop publisher to put together.  So why do they get to have all the fun?  I wanted to see how AI would do at creating some 1980s style newspaper flyers for EMToa.st.   They actually turned out pretty good. 

Here are the Full Page PRINTABLE Versions!  Feel free to print up to your work computer and leave there, or dump a few in the breakroom.  Maybe staple one over the flea market sign on the telephone pole at the bottom of the street.
