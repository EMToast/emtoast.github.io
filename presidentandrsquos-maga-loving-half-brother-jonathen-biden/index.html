President's MAGA Loving Half Brother Jonathen Biden

By EMToast Staff
October 30, 2021

Joe Biden has an evil twin brother!  Not really, but turns out he’s got the next best thing—a MAGA loving evil half-brother.

Johathen Biden, the illegitimate step bro from Scranton has an odd way of endearing himself to his POTUS big bro.

 

He proudly wears a Make America Great Again red cap everywhere he goes.

  • He posts death threats and racist comments on twitter.

  • Believes the covid vaccine has changed his brother’s DNA and personality.
