Mother Allegedly Commands Children Kill Your Father!

By EMToast Staff
December 5, 2008

47 year old man was married to his 40 year old wife for almost 20 years and they have a 17 year old son and a 16 year old daughter.  The husband is an alcoholic and got into the habit of gambling, every time he loses money or gets drunk, he'd come home and verbally abuse his wife and beat up the wife and kids.

 

One night the husband came home and found the family watching tv in the living room.  He yelled, ”How dare you watch tv, I'm the one who is paying for the utility and the rent!”  He dragged his wife to the kitchen and started beating her with a baseball bat.  The son saw it and went to get his bb gun and started shooting his father with it, saying, “I'm grown up now, I can protect mom!”  But his bb gun got stuck and he got pissed throwing the bb gun at his father.

The mother handed a knife to the son and stood in the doorway wouldn't let the father get out, the son started cutting the father with the knife, and the daughter later on came in with an iron pipe and beat her father's head with it too.  The father finally escaped and went to the police claiming his family was trying to kill him.  He also said his wife said to the son "kill your father!" when she handed him the knife.  The police arrested the mother, son and daughter after they admitted attacking the father but the mother denied telling her son to kill his father.  They also accused the father of beating up the mother but the police could not find the baseball bat so no arrest of the father at this moment.
