EMToast: The Digital Surrealism Era (2016–2018) | Podcast Deep Dive

By EMToast Staff
July 6, 2026

EMToast: The Digital Surrealism Era (2016–2018) continues the chronological deep dive into EMToast history, exploring the surreal humor, fake news stories, experimental AI imagery, bizarre fictional memoirs, and internet absurdity that defined the site during 2016 through 2018.

In this episode, we revisit another strange chapter of EMToast, from Crystal Windex soda and the mysterious Godzilla Resurgence poster to Oliver: A Humanzee Memoir, Soviet blood parasites disguised as miniature cockroaches, The Veggie Lion, and some of EMToast's earliest experiments with AI-generated imagery.

As internet culture evolved, so did EMToast. These years blended satire, surreal storytelling, early artificial intelligence, and nostalgic web humor into a uniquely strange snapshot of the late-blog era, capturing a time when the internet still felt unpredictable, experimental, and wonderfully weird.
